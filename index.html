I'm Game – Movie Information, Story, Posters & Latest Updates

I'm Game is one of the most anticipated Indian films of 2026. Starring Dulquer Salmaan in a role that promises to redefine his on-screen persona, this sports action thriller from Wayfarer Films has generated enormous excitement since its first poster dropped in early 2025. Directed by Nahas Hidhayath — the filmmaker behind the critically acclaimed RDX (2023) — I'm Game is built on high-octane ambition: 156 shooting days, over 100 locations, five language releases, and a theatrical debut timed for the Onam festival season on 20 August 2026.

Working Title

I'm Game began its journey under the working title DQ40 — a nod to the fact that this is Dulquer Salmaan's 40th film as a lead actor. The title was unveiled on 3 March 2025, accompanied by a striking first-look poster designed by Johnnie Walker that immediately went viral on social media.

The project represents a significant escalation in scale for both Nahas Hidhayath and Wayfarer Films. With a cast spanning some of Indian cinema's most versatile performers, a technically accomplished crew, and a pan-India multilingual release strategy, I'm Game is positioning itself as a landmark action entertainment experience.

I'm Game Story

🎬

I'm Game is described as a sports action thriller. The story, written by director Nahas Hidhayath, centers on Dan John — the character played by Dulquer Salmaan — who finds himself navigating a world of high-stakes danger, loyalty, and conflict.

The film's visual language, as revealed through its official posters, suggests a stylish, tension-filled narrative that blends the precision of a strategic mind with the raw violence of an action thriller. Playing cards, firearms, and a mood of calculated menace appear prominently — hinting at a story where the stakes are life-or-death and every move matters.

The screenplay was crafted by Sajeer Baba, Bilal Moidu, and Ismail Aboobacker, with dialogues written by Adarsh Sukumaran and Shabas Rasheed. The ensemble cast, which includes characters played by Mysskin (confirmed as an antagonist), Antony Varghese, and Kayadu Lohar, suggests a multi-layered narrative with a rich supporting world around the lead.

Common questions about I'm Game, answered with verified information.

I'm Game is an Indian Malayalam-language sports action thriller film directed by Nahas Hidhayath. The story, written by Nahas Hidhayath, follows Dan John (played by Dulquer Salmaan) through a world of high-stakes action and conflict. The film features a large ensemble cast and was described during production as a sports-based thriller with intense action sequences choreographed by Anbariv.

I'm Game stars Dulquer Salmaan as the lead (playing Dan John). The ensemble cast includes Kayadu Lohar (female lead), Antony Varghese (as Vicky), Mysskin (as Toni, the antagonist — marking his Malayalam debut), Samyuktha Viswanathan, Kathir, Vinay Forrt, and Parth Tiwari.

I'm Game is scheduled for a theatrical release on 20 August 2026, coinciding with the Onam festival weekend. It will be released simultaneously in Malayalam, Tamil, Telugu, Hindi, and Kannada.
